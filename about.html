About Our

Company

DA International is a Dubai-based interior finishing and contracting firm specializing in turnkey interior design and hospitality projects. We offer comprehensive architectural and interior design services, FF&E supply, and original high-end artwork. With a deep appreciation for individuality and aesthetics, we create tailored interiors that reflect each client's vision and brand identity.

Our work spans hospitality, residential, retail, and cultural sectors across the Gulf region. From luxury hotels and branded residences to multi-unit developments and curated public spaces, we bring projects to life through a fully integrated process — from design development and material sourcing to custom manufacturing and site execution.

From Vision to Reality

Dalia Adel

With over 27 years of experience in interior design and project execution, Dalia Adel has become a trusted name in the luxury hospitality and residential sectors. As Founder and CEO, she has shaped the company's identity around precision, creativity, and an uncompromising commitment to quality.

Dalia began her career on some of the region's most iconic properties, gradually growing DA International into a full-service interior contracting firm. Today her expertise extends from concept creation to turnkey delivery — including FF&E procurement, custom artwork, and site management.
