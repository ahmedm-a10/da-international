About Our

Company

DA International is a Dubai-based interior finishing and contracting firm specializing in turnkey interior design and hospitality projects. We offer comprehensive architectural and interior design services, FF&E supply, and original high-end artwork — creating tailored interiors that reflect each client's vision and brand identity.

Our work spans hospitality, residential, retail, and cultural sectors across the Gulf region, brought to life through a fully integrated process: from design development and material sourcing to custom manufacturing and on-site execution.
